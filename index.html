Shengxuan Qiu 邱圣轩

Undergraduate @ Peking University · Incoming Ph.D. @ PKU SEC Lab (Fall 2027)

I am an undergraduate student in Microelectronics Science and Engineering at the School of Electronics Engineering and Computer Science, Peking University. I am currently working with Prof. Meng Li at PKU SEC Lab and will continue there as a Ph.D. student starting in Fall 2027.

My research focuses on efficient AI systems across the model–system–hardware stack, with an emphasis on scalable reasoning, LLM serving, distributed execution, multi-agent workloads, and hardware–software co-design.

Research

Efficient LLM Reasoning & Inference-time Scaling

I study how inference-time computation can be dynamically allocated across reasoning paths. Representative work includes HyPER and SPEX, covering adaptive multi-path reasoning and speculative Tree-of-Thought execution.

LLM Serving & Multi-Agent Systems

I am interested in serving-system design for structured AI workloads, including scheduling, KV-cache management, speculative execution, synchronization, context movement, and workflow-aware optimization. My ongoing MASBench work studies composable multi-agent workloads and causal execution traces across GPU and Ascend platforms.

AI Accelerators & Hardware–Software Co-design

I work on MoE acceleration and emerging memory-centric AI architectures, including 3D near-memory processing, edge GPU inference, and GPU-PIM systems. Recent projects include HDA-MoE, S²-MoE, and Tetris.
